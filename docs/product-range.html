Warehousing Pallet Solutions

Pallets built for the realities of a working warehouse — repeated forklift handling, selective racking, and block stacking three or four high. We size the deck to your rack beam spacing rather than the other way round, so loads sit square and safely supported.

Choose four-way entry for tight aisles, or two-way stringer construction where cost per unit matters more than manoeuvrability. Close-boarded decks carry drums and sacks; spaced decks keep weight and cost down for cartoned goods.

ISPM 15 Heat Treated Pallet

Any solid wood packaging crossing a border has to meet ISPM 15. Timber is heated until its core holds 56°C for a minimum of 30 continuous minutes, killing pests and larvae without chemicals, then stamped with the IPPC mark carrying the country code, producer code and the HT treatment code.

We treat in our own kilns and keep the treatment records, so the mark on your pallet is traceable. Unmarked or incorrectly marked wood is refused at the border — the cost of getting this wrong is a container sitting at port.

Compliance Details

StandardISPM 15 (IPPC)
MethodHeat Treatment (HT)
Core Temperature56°C for 30 minutes
MarkingIPPC stamp, both sides
ChemicalsNone — heat only
RecordsTreatment log provided

Plywood Pallet & Boxes

Plywood is manufactured using heat, pressure and adhesive, which means it falls outside the scope of ISPM 15 altogether — no heat treatment, no IPPC stamp, no fumigation paperwork. For regular exporters that removes an entire step from the process.

It is also considerably lighter than solid timber for the same strength, which matters when freight is priced by weight. Smooth faces take stencilling and labels cleanly, and collapsible designs fold flat for economical return or storage.
