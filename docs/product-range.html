Precision-Engineered Wooden Packaging for Every Need

From delicate solar panels to heavy industrial machinery, Erawat Enterprise manufactures wooden packaging solutions that meet the exact specifications of your product and your supply chain. Every piece is made to order — no standard boxes, no compromises.

Bestseller

Solar Panel Wooden Boxes

Our flagship product — precision-engineered wooden crates and boxes designed specifically for solar photovoltaic (PV) modules. Whether you're shipping 72-cell polycrystalline panels, half-cut bifacial modules, or BIPV glass panels, we engineer the right box for your product.

Internal foam padding, corner protectors, anti-vibration mounts, and dividers ensure zero movement during transit. ISPM 15 certified timber means no fumigation delays at customs.

Export Wooden Pallets

Our ISPM 15 compliant export pallets are built to handle the rigours of international freight — whether shipped by sea, air, or road. Available in Euro, standard, and custom sizes, our pallets are engineered for optimal load distribution and forklift compatibility.

All pallets are heat-treated in our registered kilns and stamped with the official IPPC mark — accepted in 180+ countries for unrestricted import.

Industrial Wooden Crates

Heavy-duty open-frame or closed wooden crates for machinery, engines, compressors, and industrial equipment. Our structural engineering team designs crates that safely contain loads of any weight and size, with integral lifting points and moisture-resistant treatment.

Crates can be built with skids for forklift access, with internal blocking and bracing to prevent load shift, and with moisture barriers for long sea voyages.
