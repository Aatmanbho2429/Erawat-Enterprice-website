End-to-End Packaging Services

More Than Just Wooden Boxes

Erawat Enterprise offers a comprehensive suite of packaging services — from initial design and engineering through manufacturing, ISPM 15 certification, and delivery. We are your one-stop packaging partner, eliminating the need to coordinate multiple vendors.

Custom Wooden Packaging Manufacturing

01

Precision Manufacturing at Scale

Our core service: manufacturing custom wooden packaging to your exact specifications. We work with seasoned hardwood, pinewood, and commercial plywood — selecting the right material for your product's weight, fragility, and transit environment.

Our CNC woodworking machinery ensures every dimension is cut to within ±1mm of specification. Assembly is performed by skilled carpenters using proper fasteners, with quality checks at every stage.

Wooden boxes, crates, pallets, frames
Any dimension — micro to mega
Minimum order: 10 units
Bulk orders: 10,000+ units per month
7–21 day lead time depending on volume
Quality inspection certificate included

ISPM 15 Heat Treatment & Certification

Our NPPO-registered heat-treatment facility is the heart of our export packaging service. All wooden packaging material is treated in our kilns at a core temperature of 56°C for a minimum of 30 minutes, eliminating all wood pests and meeting ISPM 15 requirements.

After treatment, each unit is stamped with the official IPPC mark — including the India country code, our producer code, and the HT symbol. Full documentary evidence is provided for customs clearance.

Official IPPC mark (XX/INDIA/00/HT)
Treatment logs & certificates provided

Packaging Design & Engineering

03

Engineering-Led Packaging Design

Not sure what kind of packaging your product needs? Our in-house engineering team will assess your product, analyse the transit risks (vibration, stacking, humidity), and design a packaging solution from the ground up using CAD software.

We produce 2D drawings and 3D models for client approval before any manufacturing begins. Prototype units can be built for testing and refinement before bulk production.

Product risk assessment
CAD design & 3D modelling
Material selection recommendation
Prototype fabrication & testing
Transit simulation testing
Documentation for insurance claims
