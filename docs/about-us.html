Our Journey

Building Trust, One Box at a Time

Founded in 2005, Erawat Enterprise began as a small workshop in Gujarat with a single mission: to provide the wooden packaging industry with a level of quality and precision it had never seen before. What started with a handful of skilled craftsmen and basic machinery has grown into one of Gujarat's most trusted and comprehensive wooden packaging manufacturers.

Over nearly two decades, we have invested continuously in infrastructure, machinery, and human capital. Today, our state-of-the-art manufacturing facility spans thousands of square metres and houses CNC woodworking machines, precision saws, and NPPO-registered heat-treatment kilns. Our workforce of over 150 skilled professionals brings deep expertise in packaging engineering, quality control, and logistics.

The solar energy boom in India opened a new chapter for Erawat. Solar panel manufacturers quickly discovered that off-the-shelf wooden boxes were inadequate for protecting their fragile modules — and that ISPM 15 compliance was non-negotiable for export orders. We responded by developing specialised solar-panel packaging solutions that are now the benchmark for the industry.
